# Consumer startups: personal AI dashboards, AI chiefs of staff, generative personal workspaces (as of 2026-09-26)

Method: about 22 web searches and fetches, run on 2026-09-26. Where I could, I used primary or near-primary sources (TechCrunch, company blogs, CNBC, Bloomberg). Items marked [aggregator] come from SEO or review blogs and need checking before anyone relies on them. Items marked [stale] are older than about 6 months.

## Q1. AI chief-of-staff and proactive assistant startups (consumer and prosumer)

### Takeaway
In 2026 the funding and attention went to **messaging-first proactive assistants** (Instinct, Poke, Lindy, OpenClaw and its hosting ecosystem). None of them gives the user a persistent visual workspace; the interface is a text thread, sometimes with a companion app. The early leaders have mostly exited (Poke to Cognition, Limitless to Meta, Manus through Meta and back out). Instinct is the category's best-funded independent player.

### Cited Findings

**Instinct (Spear Street Technology). Alive, private beta. The strongest direct threat.**
- A personal AI assistant you text or call. It connects to the user's apps and devices and runs errands: road trips, groceries, concert tickets, cancelling subscriptions, wedding planning. Raised $350M in total, including a $250M Series B co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark at a $2.5B valuation. Announced 2026-08-26. The founder is 23-year-old Noah Shinn and the company is about one year old. It has drawn privacy criticism ("overly generous permissions", invasive terms of use). — [TechCrunch 2026-08-26](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/viral-ai-startup-instinct-has-raised-350-million-at-a-2-5-billion-valuation/)
- Connects to email, messages, screen, audio and location, then texts or calls the user proactively. It recently added its own email addresses and outbound phone calls. It is reportedly in talks at a $10B valuation. — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/startups/2026/instinct-ai-assistant-targets-10-billion-dollar-valuation/); [search summary of Vellum/CellCog explainers, aggregator](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/official-instinct-breakdown)
- Invite-only and free during the beta, with no announced pricing, as of September 2026. — [aggregator: spinnable.ai](https://www.spinnable.ai/blog/what-is-instinct-ai-guide)
- Axios (2026-09-20) grouped Instinct with OpenAI, Meta ("Muse"), Grok and Apple in an "AI assistant race". I could not read the full text (HTTP 403). — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/20/ai-assistant-openai-meta-muse-instinct-grok-apple)

**Poke (The Interaction Company of California). Acquired by Cognition in July 2026.**
- An AI assistant that works through iMessage, SMS, Telegram and WhatsApp and handles travel, health, finance, scheduling and education. It is built around personality and humour. More than 100M messages in three months, "hundreds of thousands" of users, and the first AI agent approved for Apple Messages for Business (June 2026). Cognition bought it in July 2026 at a "low nine figures" valuation. Poke keeps running through 2026, with integration experiments planned for 2027. — [TechCrunch 2026-07-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/)
- Funding: a $15M seed (General Catalyst, the Collison brothers), then $10M in April 2026 led by Spark Capital and General Catalyst. Reported valuation $300M. — [search summary of Crunchbase/Tracxn, aggregator](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/poke-acfb). Note that $300M is higher than the "low nine figures" exit valuation. The two figures are not reconciled.

**Lindy. Alive. Pivoted from agent builder to consumer "AI executive assistant" in early 2026.**
- Plans: Plus $49.99/month, Pro $99.99/month, Max $199.99/month, a 7-day trial and no free tier. Relaunched as a consumer-facing AI executive assistant in early 2026, replacing its agent-builder pricing. About $50M raised; Battery Ventures led the largest round. Founder Flo Crivello. — [aggregator: getmacha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/lindy-ai-pricing-explained); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/lindy). The search headline gives the range as "$29.99 to $199.99", which conflicts with the body text. Check lindy.ai/pricing.
- Pitch: always reachable by text, handles the inbox, meetings and calendar proactively, and claims to give users "2+ hours back every day". — [Zapier blog](https://zapier.com/blog/lindy-pricing/)

**Fambot. New; beta launched 2026-09-01.**
- An "AI chief of staff" for parents. It connects email, calendars and WhatsApp groups and turns them into daily checklists and previews of upcoming events. Available on iOS, Android and web, and you can also text it. Free in beta, with planned pricing around the cost of a Netflix subscription. Raised $3.5M pre-seed (NextView, Baukunst, Correlation, Karman, Founders Network). TechCrunch names Poke and Instinct as comparables. — [TechCrunch 2026-09-01](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/fambot-introduces-an-ai-chief-of-staff-for-families/)
- This is the closest funded analogue to "aggregate sources into persistent views", but the views are fixed (checklists and events), not built by an agent.

**OpenClaw and its hosting ecosystem. Open source, massive adoption.**
- A self-hosted personal assistant that runs on your own machine or a VPS, connects to more than 23 messaging platforms, and lets you choose the model. It went from "Clawdbot" (November 2025) to "Moltbot" to "OpenClaw" (January 2026) and reached 356k GitHub stars in three months. — [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/resources/articles/what-is-openclaw); [openclaw.ai](https://openclaw.ai/)
- Businesses have grown around it: one-click hosts (Clawhosters, ClawHost, exoclaw, Run My Claw), Claw Mart (reportedly $106k/month selling configurations), Roofclaw ($60k/month shipping pre-configured MacBook Airs), and Blink Claw hosting at about $22/month including LLM costs. — [aggregator: blog.mean.ceo, Sep 2026](https://blog.mean.ceo/openclaw-news-september-2026/). The revenue figures are unverified.
- An article headlined "Poke is OpenClaw for normies". — [Sources.news](https://sources.news/p/poke-openclaw-for-normies)

**Martin (YC S23). Status unclear.**
- A voice and text AI assistant with a "custom memory architecture". $2M seed. Founders Dawson Chen and Ethan Hou. — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/these-yale-and-berkeley-dropouts-just-raised-2-million-to-build-an-ai-assistant-that-could-rival-openai) [stale, 2024]
- The YC company page at ycombinator.com/companies/martin now reads "Letterbook: AI-native customer support platform". — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/martin). **Inference:** Martin appears to have pivoted to B2B customer support. Confirm before stating it as fact.

**Dume.ai, Saner.ai, Mem**
- Dume.ai: an assistant available by phone, WhatsApp, browser and desktop that triages email, sends morning briefs and finishes long tasks. — [dume.ai](https://www.dume.ai/vs/martin-ai)
- Saner.ai: an ADHD-friendly assistant for capturing, organising and retrieving information. — [saner.ai](https://www.saner.ai/blogs/best-martin-alternatives)
- Mem: listed as a knowledge-management competitor of Saner. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/sanerai/alternatives-competitors)
- I found no 2026 funding, pricing or traction data for any of the three.

**Other 2026 names**
- Orchid: an executive assistant in iMessage that texts you your day each morning, drafts replies and books meetings, with approval first. Bond: an AI chief of staff for CEOs. Pally, Hey Noah, Toyo and AirJelly are listed as 2026 entrants. — [Product Hunt "AI Chief of Staff" category](https://www.producthunt.com/categories/ai-chief-of-staff) (via search summary; not fetched)
- YC S26: **Moonshot**, a proactive iPhone assistant that listens ambiently, with permission, to track commitments and patterns. Also Clice (personal assistants that learn your communication style) and Egoist Machines (user-owned personal context). — [Extruct S26 list](https://www.extruct.ai/data-room/ycombinator-companies-s26/); [Forbes 2026-09-03](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dariashunina/2026/09/03/meet-the-yc-startups-betting-on-what-comes-next/)

**Platform incumbents (context only; these are not startups)**
- OpenAI launched ChatGPT Pulse on 2025-09-26 for Pro users: proactive daily research cards built from chats and connected calendars. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-pulse/)
- **Pulse was shut down on 2026-06-17.** The given reasons: stale topics ("relevance decay"), poor timing of interruptions, and cards to read instead of actions. It was replaced by user-configured **scheduled tasks** (at most 10 active) and **web-monitoring tasks**. — [aggregator: prowlo.com](https://prowlo.com/blog/chatgpt-pulse-shut-down). Confirm against OpenAI release notes.
- A leaked ChatGPT Pro upgrade screen (2026-09-25) mentions "o, your always-on assistant". — [aggregator: progressiverobot.com](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/09/26/openai-always-on-assistant-o/). Unconfirmed.

### Inferences
- The money-backed "chief of staff" pattern is **chat-first with no UI**: text or iMessage, proactive nudges, actions taken on your behalf. The founder's idea of **persistent, agent-maintained visual tabs** is a real difference from Instinct, Poke and Lindy.
- OpenAI's retreat from inferred proactivity (Pulse) to **explicit, user-defined scheduled and monitoring jobs** supports the founder's model: the user names the tab or job in natural language, and the agent keeps it up to date.
- Big labs (OpenAI "o", Meta Muse, Apple) are moving into always-on assistants in H2 2026. That puts pressure on any generic assistant pitch.

### Gaps
- No pricing or traction numbers for Dume, Saner, Mem, Orchid, Bond or Moonshot.
- Sunday, Friday and Sesame: I did not research their 2026 status within the call budget.
- Axios's full list of assistant launches was paywalled.

## Q2. Generative personal software ("the agent builds your app or workspace")

### Takeaway
Of all the products in this research, the closest to "the agent builds persistent tabs from your context" are **Google Labs Disco/GenTabs** (experimental, free) and the open-source **Hermes Agent "Live Dashboard"** skill. The funded "AI workspace" companies (Genspark, Manus, Taskade) aim at work output, not a personal life dashboard.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Disco / GenTabs** (Google Labs, announced 2025-12-11): an experimental browser in which Gemini 3 reads your open tabs and chat history and writes an interactive web app ("GenTab") on the fly. macOS waitlist only; Windows planned for 2026; free with no pricing. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/gentabs-gemini-3/); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/11/google-disco-gentab-browser/)
- **Hermes Agent Live Dashboard** (Nous Research, open source): a skill that turns a request like "make a dashboard for our visa applications, update it daily from the email threads and the case-status site" into a persistent status page that refreshes itself. This is almost exactly the founder's concept, packaged as a feature of an open-source agent. — [Hermes docs](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/skills/optional/productivity/productivity-live-dashboard)
- **Genspark**: a $275M Series B at a $1.25B post-money valuation (November 2025), about $545M raised in total. It reportedly passed $200M ARR within 12 months of launch. On 2026-03-12 it launched "Claw", its first "AI Employee" product, alongside AI Workspace 3.0, spanning research, docs, slides, data and meeting notes. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251120036880/en/Genspark-Raises-$275M-Series-B-Launches-AI-Workspace-to-Put-Busywork-on-Autopilot); ARR and Claw details are from an [aggregator (taskade blog)](https://www.taskade.com/blog/best-genspark-alternatives)
- **Manus**: Meta agreed to buy it for about $2B in December 2025. Beijing ordered the deal unwound in April 2026 on national-security grounds. Meta cut off data access in June 2026. On 2026-08-11 Manus said it would "soon return to operating as an independent company". — [CNBC 2026-08-11](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/manus-china-meta-acquisition.html); [Bloomberg 2026-06-11](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/meta-severs-manus-data-access-after-china-orders-buyout-unwound); [TechCrunch 2026-06-13](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/meta-reportedly-moves-to-unwind-2b-manus-deal-after-beijings-demand/)
- **Taskade Genesis** (launched October 2025): describe an app in plain English and get a shareable web app, with AI agents, List/Board/Calendar/Table views, scheduled updates and 100+ integrations. From about $8–10 per month. — [aggregator: coin360](https://coin360.com/review/taskade); [Taskade blog](https://www.taskade.com/blog/ai-dashboard-examples)
- Show HN, September 2026: "Personal Agent Dashboard", a local dashboard with an email-to-task agent pipeline (a hobby project). — [GitHub](https://github.com/matt-huesman/personal-agent-dashboard/pull/1). Also DAC, an open-source "dashboard as code for agents" (May 2026). — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47949066)

### Inferences
- The "agent writes a UI for you" capability is turning into a commodity feature (GenTabs, Hermes, Taskade Genesis, Claude artifacts). The defensible part is probably **persistence plus scheduled refresh plus connected personal data**, not generating the UI.
- I found no funded consumer startup whose core product is multiple agent-maintained persistent tabs with background jobs. This is based on searches only; there could be stealth or small Product Hunt launches I missed.

### Gaps
- I did not cover Tana, Capacities, Reflect, Anytype, Coda/Superhuman, Airtable Omni, Val Town, Lovable/Bolt/Replit personal use, Gumloop, Zapier Agents or n8n, because of the tool-call budget.
- I did not scan Product Hunt leaderboards in detail. The digest feeds [agents-radar](https://github.com/duanyytop/agents-radar/issues/3504) exist for a follow-up.

## Q3. Monitoring and aggregation agents, and failures or pivots (lessons)

### Takeaway
Website-change monitoring is a mature, cheap category (about $10–15/month per individual) that has added AI features. The failures cluster around **dedicated hardware** and around **inferred proactivity without action**. The survivors turn into software or are acquired.

### Cited Findings
- Visualping: a free tier with 5 monitors and about 150 checks per month, paid plans from about $10–14/month, and an AI layer on top of change detection. Distill.io: a free extension, with cloud plans from $15/month. — [aggregator: pagecrawl.io](https://pagecrawl.io/blog/best-ai-website-monitoring-tools); [visualping.io](https://visualping.io/)
- **Limitless/Rewind**: Meta acquired it on 2025-12-05. Pendant sales stopped, the Rewind Mac app turned off capture on 2025-12-19, the Pendant gets one year of support, and service ended immediately in the EU and UK. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/12/05/rewind-limitless-meta-acquisition/); [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/meta-acquires-ai-device-startup-limitless/)
- **Humane**: HP bought its IP for about $116M in February 2025, and the AI Pin was discontinued on 2025-02-28. — [search summary of digitalapplied, aggregator](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-product-failures-2026-sora-humane-rabbit-lessons) [stale]
- **Rabbit**: staff reported unpaid wages and a strike. OS3, generally available 2026-09-22, is a cloud agent that drives Windows, Mac and Linux machines. The r1 is still supported but no longer made or required. Around September 2024 it had about 100k buyers and about 5k DAU (about 5%). — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/110256-rabbit-employees-they-havent-paid-months-but-r1.html); [aggregator: progressiverobot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/09/23/desktop-ai-agent-rabbit-os3-no-r1-needed/)
- **ChatGPT Pulse** was shut down in June 2026 over relevance decay, interruption timing and passive output (see Q1). — [prowlo.com](https://prowlo.com/blog/chatgpt-pulse-shut-down)
- **Martin** appears to have pivoted from a consumer assistant to B2B support ("Letterbook"). — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/martin)
- **Lindy** pivoted in the opposite direction, from an agent-builder platform to a consumer executive assistant (early 2026). — [getmacha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/lindy-ai-complete-guide)

### Inferences
- Lessons for the founder:
  1. Don't build hardware.
  2. Proactivity the user didn't ask for decays into spam, so let the user define each tab and job explicitly (as OpenAI now does).
  3. Pair digests with actions, not just cards to read.
  4. Privacy and permission scope is a live liability; Instinct is already being criticised for it.
  5. The consumer exits so far have been acqui-hires into bigger platforms (Poke to Cognition, Limitless to Meta), and the one large one (Manus) ran into regulators.
- Both the monitoring tools and OpenAI's web-monitoring tasks are close to free, so "watch sites I visit" will not carry pricing on its own. The value has to come from cross-source synthesis in a persistent view.

### Gaps
- Browse AI's 2026 pricing and consumer positioning were not found.
- I did not research AI news-digest or "feeds" startups (e.g. Particle, Artifact successors).
- Sunday, Friday, Sesame, and Rewind successors other than Limitless were not covered.
